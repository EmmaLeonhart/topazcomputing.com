Arkhos Winter

Co-founder · Topaz Computing

Arkhos Winter, also known as Arcos, is a co-founder of Topaz Computing and of SovGrid.

About

Arkhos Winter co-founded Topaz Computing with Emma Leonhart. Together they also co-founded SovGrid, a subsidiary of Topaz building solar power for Canadian data centres, so they can grow without straining the local grid or burning natural gas.
