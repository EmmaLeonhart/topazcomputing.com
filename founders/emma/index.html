Emma Leonhart is an AI researcher and the founder of Topaz Computing.

About

Emma Leonhart founded Topaz Computing and leads its research and engineering. She is based in Vancouver, British Columbia. She holds a Bachelor's in Economics and an Associate's in Computer Science, and taught herself the rest of the field.
